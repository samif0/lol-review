Libraries in ffmpeg.exe
=======================

ffmpeg.exe is one statically linked program. Besides FFmpeg itself it contains
the libraries below, compiled by BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds at release
autobuild-2026-08-31-13-27 (variant win64-gpl-8.1). Each entry names the
source repository and the exact revision that was compiled (from scripts.d/ in
the build scripts at that release), its license, and where its license texts
are in this folder. Header-only entries are interfaces compiled into
ffmpeg.exe; the libraries behind them are not shipped with Revu.
