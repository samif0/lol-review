Your recordings and notes are stored on your PC.

Start with the recording

Every match review opens on its recording. Jump to the 10 seconds before each death, save clips and notes on the moments that matter, then write one takeaway for your next game.

Built-in recording is in beta and stays off until you turn it on in Settings. It records Ranked Solo/Duo with League in Borderless mode and needs an NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card with hardware H.264 encoding. Already record with Ascent? Connect its folder instead.

Practice one thing

Choose one skill to work on and give it a rule for success. Set it as your focus before you queue, and Revu follows it across the games where you practiced it, from Exploring to Ready.

See what repeats

Mark the moments you want to improve. When the same mistake shows up in two or more games, Revu groups those moments so you can compare them. Once it keeps repeating across three games, watch them back to back and decide what to change.

Your next game starts with your last one.
