Review every ranked game, alone or with your coach.

Revu records your League games and helps you pick one thing to practice. Share a clip or a game as a link your coach can draw on, and set stop rules that cancel the queue when you said you would stop.

Latest version · Windows 10/11

Send a game to your coach

Share a clip or a full game from Revu and you get a link. Your coach opens it in a browser, with nothing to install and no account.

On that page they draw on the video, bookmark moments, clip parts of the game and write a note on each. Copy link with notes puts all of it into one link to send back. Open it, and their notes and drawings are on your game.

Sharing needs a Revu account, signed in by email, and a link stays up for 3 days. Any account can share clips. Full-game sharing is switched on one account at a time for now. A shared video shows other players' names and in-game chat. A coach's notes stay in their browser and in the links they copy, and are never uploaded.

Stop when you said you would

Write your own stop rules: a loss streak, a daily game limit, an hour of the night, or days you don't play. Each one can carry a plan for what you will do instead.

Turn on Hard stop for a rule and, while that rule is tripped, Revu cancels your ranked queue search, the same as pressing Cancel in the client. If a match has already been found, Revu leaves it to you: it never accepts or declines a ready check.

Hard stop is off until you turn it on for a rule, and it holds Ranked Solo/Duo and Flex only. You can queue anyway after a five-minute wait, and Revu counts each time you do.

Start with the recording

When a game has a recording, its review starts there. Jump to any moment or straight to a death, save clips and bookmarks with notes, then write one thing to take into your next game.

Built-in recording is in beta and stays off until you turn it on in Settings. It records Ranked Solo/Duo with League in Borderless mode and needs an NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card with hardware H.264 encoding.

Practice one thing

Choose one skill to work on and say what success looks like. Make it your priority, and Revu follows it across the games where you practiced it, from Exploring to Ready.

See what repeats

Save clips and bookmarks to an objective and mark the ones to improve. When they show up in two or more games, Revu groups them into a mistake trend you can compare. Once a trend has four moments across three games, watch them back to back and decide what to change.

Your next game starts with your last one.
