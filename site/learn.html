Desktop app

Every game, Revu'd.

The browser tool reviews one game. The desktop app reviews every game. It links local VODs to your matches, keeps a session log, and shows the habits you're building across patches and splits.

  • Review your VODs.

    Watch local recordings of your matches with timestamped notes and clips.

  • Practice one thing at a time.

    Track objectives and session rules across sessions and seasons.

  • Narrate your clips.

    Talk over any clip in the desktop app and share it with captions and a transcript.

  • Build a session habit.

    Set an intent before you queue and see how each session went at a glance.
